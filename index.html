How I Work

Research to reach

The same four moves, whether it's an episode or a campaign.

I work in high-ownership, fast-paced, fast-moving environments — where the brief shifts, and the call still has to get made.

01
Research first
Understand the guest, the category, and what the audience already knows. The question nobody has asked yet is in there somewhere.
02
Shape the script
Topic, question order, and the spine of the story. What earns the first sixty seconds, and what earns the next forty minutes.
03
Cut for the platform
One episode becomes a dozen assets. Each cut built for where it lands, not resized and reposted.
04
Read the numbers
Retention, hook rate, CPV. What the data says goes back into the next brief, not into a deck nobody opens.
